Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version, for parents

  • At home, your child can use the app with no account and no sign-up.
  • At home, your child's activity — tasks, feelings, stars, badges — stays on your own device. It is not sent to us.
  • In a classroom, your child's records are stored securely and only their teacher can see them.
  • You can export or erase everything at any time.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • We never sell data, and we don't build profiles of children.

Full policy

1. Who this is for

Focus Bridge is an educational tool for young children, including special-needs elementary students. It can be used at home by a family, or in a classroom under the direction of a school and teacher. We designed it to collect as little personal information as possible.

2. Children's data (COPPA)

Focus Bridge is made for children, so we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • At home, Focus Bridge does not collect personal information from children. Everything stays on the family's device (see section 3).
  • In a classroom, a child's information is collected only when a school directs the use of Focus Bridge for an educational purpose. The school may consent on parents' behalf, as COPPA allows, and a supervising adult confirms that consent when each child joins a class. The information is used only for that educational purpose, and only as the school directs.

3. Home mode (no account, on-device)

Home mode requires no account, no school, and no sign-up. The app does not ask for a child's name (an optional first name may be entered and is kept only on the device), and it does not send a child's information to us: activity is stored in the app on the family's own device. A grown-ups gate protects the settings area, where a parent can export or erase all of it at any time.

4. Student records and schools (FERPA)

When we work with a school, student information may be an education record under FERPA. We then act as a school official with a legitimate educational interest, under the school's direction, and under a data processing agreement where the school requires one. We do not use education records for anything except providing Focus Bridge to that school.

5. What we collect in a classroom

  • From students: a first name only (or an ID instead, at the teacher's choice) and a chosen picture. We do not ask for last names.
  • From using the app: task completion, emotion check-ins, rewards and badges, stories read, and when activities happened, plus the notes and social stories a teacher writes.
  • From teachers (adults): a name, and a username and password for signing in (no email address is needed). Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Teachers also give their school's name and ZIP code, which we use only to count how many schools and regions use Focus Bridge. These are never shown to students or families.
  • We do not collect home addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, or biometric data, and we use no advertising identifiers.

6. How we use information

Only to run Focus Bridge: to show schedules and activities, and to track progress for teachers and parents. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

7. Who else is involved

In Home mode, nothing is shared: the data does not leave the device. In a classroom, a child's records are visible to their teacher, and to the child on their own signed-in device. A few service providers help us run Focus Bridge, and they handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores classroom data and teacher sign-ins.
  • Netlify hosts the website and the app.
  • unpkg and Google Fonts deliver some of the app's code and its lettering. Like any website, they see a device's internet (IP) address when the app loads, but they receive no names or records.

Read-aloud uses the speech voice built into the device or browser. On some devices, the device maker's speech service may process the words being read. We also disclose information when the law requires it.

8. Storage, security, and retention

Home-mode data is stored in the app on the family's device. Classroom data is stored with Supabase and is always sent over encrypted connections. Access rules in the database let teachers reach only their own classrooms, and children only their own records.

We keep classroom data while the classroom is in use. It is deleted as soon as a teacher deletes the child's record or their own account, a child deletes their own data, or a parent or school asks us to delete it. No way of storing data is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Your choices: reviewing, correcting, and deleting

Parents and schools can review a child's information, correct it, have it deleted, and withdraw consent, which stops any further collection. Most of this can be done in the app:

  • At home, a parent can view, export, or erase everything in the Grown-ups Area.
  • In a classroom, a teacher can view, export, or delete a child's record from the dashboard. A child can download or delete their own data from their settings, behind a grown-up check.
  • A teacher can permanently delete their own account, and every classroom it owns, from the dashboard's Settings tab.

Deleting a child's record also deletes their notes and consent records. For anything else, or if you can't use the app, contact us (section 11). The Delete Your Data page explains each option step by step.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. We will tell schools and parents about any important change that affects children's data.
