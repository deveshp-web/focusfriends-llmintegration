Delete Your Account & Data

This page works whether or not the app is installed.

The short version

Every deletion option below is immediate and permanent — nothing is held in a "trash" or recovery period. Which steps apply to you depends on how the app was used; find your situation below.

🏠 HOME USE A parent using Focus Bridge at home

Home use has no account and nothing is stored on our servers — your child's activity lives only in the app on your device. To delete it:

  1. Open Focus Bridge and go to the Grown-ups Area (behind the parental check).
  2. Under Your Data, tap Delete all data, then tap it again to confirm.

This immediately erases the child's profile, schedule, and progress from the device. You can also achieve the same result by uninstalling the app or clearing site data for it in your browser.

🎒 STUDENT A student in a teacher's classroom

A student can erase their own record at any time:

  1. In the app, open Settings (the gear icon) while signed in.
  2. Tap Delete my data.
  3. A grown-up check appears (this prevents a young child from doing this by accident) — a grown-up completes it to confirm.

This permanently deletes that student's record — name, progress, badges, and activity — from the classroom's database. A teacher can also delete a student's record for them from the teacher dashboard.

📊 TEACHER A teacher with a classroom account

A teacher can permanently delete their entire account and everything it owns:

  1. Log in and open the dashboard's Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the red Delete my account section and tap Delete my account and all my data.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently delete.

This immediately and permanently removes: every classroom you created, all students enrolled in them and their progress, teacher notes, consent records, activity logs, and your login itself. Students enrolled in other teachers' classrooms are never affected.
